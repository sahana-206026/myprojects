About BenefitBridge

Making government welfare schemes simple, accessible, and reachable — especially for rural communities.

🚧 The Problem

Many eligible citizens miss government schemes due to lack of awareness, complex eligibility rules, and scattered information sources — especially in rural areas.

💡 Our Solution

BenefitBridge analyzes basic user details and instantly shows only the schemes they are eligible for — no confusion, no searching.

⚙️ How It Works

  • User enters simple personal details
  • System checks eligibility logic
  • Relevant schemes are displayed
  • Users can save schemes for later

🌍 Our Impact

We aim to reduce information gaps, empower citizens, and ensure no eligible individual is left behind from government welfare benefits.
